Returns & Refunds

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Return and refund conditions depend on the product, purchase location and retail channel used for the transaction. Arbound does not currently process orders directly through arboundgroup.com.

Purchases through retail channels

For products purchased through a third-party retail channel, the return window, eligibility requirements and refund process presented at the time of purchase apply.

Product condition

Items may need to be unused, unopened or returned with original packaging depending on the product category and the applicable sales channel.

Support

If you need help identifying the appropriate return process, contact commercial@arboundgroup.com with the product and purchase details.
